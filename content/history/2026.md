REBUILT combines a variety of game elements from the past 35 years of FIRST Robotics history. For example, "Fuel" is borrowed from 2017's Steamworks, the "Hub" is borrowed from Rapid React in 2022, and the 2006 game Aim High gave us the concept of "Match Periods". 

Teams compete to score Fuel into their alliance's Hub, navigate obstacles such as the Bump and Trench, and climb the tower to earn bonus points at the end of the round.
