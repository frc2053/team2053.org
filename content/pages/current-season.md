The calendar above is the schedule. It is kept in Google Calendar rather than on
this page, which means it is right about this week even when nothing else here
has been touched in a year — so if a meeting is not on it, we are not meeting.

We normally meet in the shop at Vestal High School on Monday, Wednesday and
Friday from 5 to 8pm, and on Saturday from 8am to 1pm; build season and
competition weekends move things around, and the calendar is where that shows
up. Please be outside the front doors of the school when the meeting starts,
because after that you will have to wait for a mentor to come and let you in.
New students and their families should read the
[team handbook and rules](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1bE_2YUtZ2e0jtnDK2_itQkPTz2gczKb6DidZ5INTC1k/pub)
before their first meeting, and FIRST publishes
[the game, the rules and the event schedule](https://info.firstinspires.org/first-age)
for whatever season it currently is. Anything else — joining, visiting, bringing
us to your school — goes to
[southerntierrobotics@gmail.com](mailto:southerntierrobotics@gmail.com).
