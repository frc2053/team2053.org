Southern Tier Robotics is FIRST® Robotics Competition team 2053, based at
Vestal High School in Vestal, New York. We design, build and program a robot
every season, and we compete with it against teams from across the northeast.

## What is FIRST?

FIRST® — For Inspiration and Recognition of Science and Technology — exists to
get young people excited about science and engineering, and to put them next to
adults who do that work for a living. Its programs run from pre-kindergarten
through twelfth grade, and they are built around the same idea at every level:
students learn the engineering by doing it, not by being shown it. There is a
lot more about the organization
at [firstinspires.org](https://www.firstinspires.org/).

## What is the FIRST Robotics Competition?

The FIRST® Robotics Competition is the high school program, open to students in
grades nine through twelve, and it is the one this team competes in. Every
January, FIRST reveals a brand new game that every team in the world plays that
year. Teams then get roughly six weeks to design, build and program a robot of
about 120 pounds from nothing, and take it to a regional competition.

Matches are three robots against three. Teams are paired at random through the
qualifying rounds, and then the top eight teams pick the partners they want for
the playoffs. Win a regional with your alliance and you are invited to the world
championship, where teams from every country in the program come to compete.

## Team history

The team was founded in 2007 as Tigertronics, working out of Union-Endicott High
School. Since then it has gained and lost mentors, watched students graduate
high school and then college and go out into the workforce — and watched a fair
number of them come back to mentor. Through all of it the robots have kept
getting better.

FIRST cancelled its competition events in 2020 and 2021 because of COVID, and
during that break the team moved its home to Vestal High School. We changed the
name at the same time, to Southern Tier Robotics, because students come to us
from more than one school and the old name no longer said so. Every robot we
have built is on the [history page](/history/).
